# Resources

## Standards and guidance
- CryptoCurrency Security Standard (CCSS), CryptoCurrency Certification Consortium (C4)
- NIST SP 800-57 Part 1, Recommendation for Key Management
- NIST SP 800-61, Computer Security Incident Handling Guide
- [BIP 174: Partially Signed Bitcoin Transactions](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0174.mediawiki)
- FATF guidance on virtual assets and virtual asset service providers (2019, updated 2021)

## Tools
- [Bitcoin Core](https://bitcoincore.org) and its [multisig tutorial](https://github.com/bitcoin/bitcoin/blob/master/doc/multisig-tutorial.md)
- [Foundry](https://book.getfoundry.sh) (`anvil`, `cast`) for local EVM experiments
- [SoftHSM2](https://github.com/softhsm/SoftHSMv2) and OpenSC `pkcs11-tool` for HSM practice
- [Safe smart account contracts](https://github.com/safe-global/safe-smart-account)
- [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html) transaction isolation docs

## Papers and articles
- Canetti, Gennaro, Goldfeder, Makriyannis, Peled, "UC Non-Interactive, Proactive, Threshold ECDSA with Identifiable Aborts" (CCS 2020)
- Vitalik Buterin, ["Having a safe CEX: proof of solvency and beyond"](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2022/11/19/proof_of_solvency.html) (2022)

## Incident sources
- FBI, DC3 and Japan NPA joint statement on DMM Bitcoin (December 2024)
- FBI statement attributing the Bybit theft to TraderTraitor (February 2025), via [IC3](https://www.ic3.gov)
- Bybit and Safe incident reports with Sygnia and Verichains findings (2025)
- WazirX and Liminal post-incident statements (2024)
